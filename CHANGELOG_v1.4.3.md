# Night Harvest 1.4.3

## Fixes
- **Ripe wheat is visible again.** The last growth stage pointed at an empty tile, so
  fully grown wheat looked like bare soil. Wheat now grows seed -> green shoots ->
  heading -> golden ripe wheat.
- **No more rocks inside trees.** Rocks, berry bushes and mushrooms no longer generate
  under a tree's canopy (and the map no longer shows those rocks). Existing saves keep
  their chopped trees and mined rocks.
- **Doors and gates work from both sides.** You can now open and close a door you
  built while standing behind it, not just in front of it.

## Improvements
- **Animals feel alive when they stand still.** Instead of freezing on one frame they
  breathe and, every few seconds, do something: chickens and ducks peck and ruffle
  their feathers, cows, pigs, sheep, goats and deer graze and twitch, rabbits and hares
  hop and nibble, and everyone looks around now and then. A light trot bounce was
  added while walking.
- **New logo.** The crossed sickle and shovel with the zombie hand is the new game
  icon (window, taskbar, .exe) and sits above the title on the main menu.
